App Brainstorming
===

## App ideas
1. Grocery list
2. Habit tracker
3. Recipe finder


## Evaluate and categorize

### Grocery list
Category: Productivity / Lifestyle

Mobile: Designed for quick, on-the-go access while shopping; mobile-first interface with simple input and editing.

Story: Helps users stay organized by creating and managing grocery shopping lists, avoiding forgotten items and overspending.

Market: Anyone who shops for groceries — individuals, families, roommates — can benefit from the convenience and organization.

Habit: Users typically engage with the app before or during a grocery trip. Power users may update lists multiple times a week, especially if they plan meals or manage household shopping.

Scope: Starts narrowly focused on simple grocery list creation. Can expand later into features like barcode scanning, collaborative lists, meal planning, and automatic inventory tracking.

### Habit Tracker
Category: Health & Fitness / Productivity

Mobile: Mobile-first, daily check-in style interface optimized for quick taps and visual habit streak tracking.

Story: Encourages users to build good habits and break bad ones by providing daily motivation, visual progress tracking, and streak-based rewards.

Market: Anyone trying to improve routines — from students to professionals to wellness enthusiasts. Covers fitness, study, mindfulness, etc.

Habit: Highly repetitive usage — users are encouraged to open the app at least once per day to check off completed habits and maintain streaks.

Scope: Starts simple with adding and checking off habits. Can expand into habit analytics, reminders, social accountability groups, or challenges.

### Recipe Finder App
Category: Food & Drink / Lifestyle

Mobile: Mobile-first experience designed to quickly search, browse, and save recipes on the go, often used while shopping or cooking.

Story: Helps users discover new meals to cook based on available ingredients, diet preferences, or inspiration needs.

Market: Home cooks, busy professionals, students, or anyone trying to eat healthier or diversify their meals.

Habit: Users might check in a few times a week — usually before cooking or grocery shopping. Could become daily usage for users who meal prep or cook frequently.

Scope: Starts focused on recipe discovery. Can expand into meal planning, personalized diet tracking, grocery list generation, and cooking timers.
